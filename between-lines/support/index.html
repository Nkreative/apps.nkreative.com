Between Lines for iPhone

Support

Between Lines helps you read the messages that matter. Paste a message from someone in your life and get a calm reading of what it might mean, how they might be feeling, and three ways you could reply: gentle, direct, or with a boundary. There's no account, and what you save stays on your iPhone.

Need help?

Email support@nkreative.com. It helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version and Between Lines' version (in your iPhone's Settings › General › iPhone Storage › Between Lines).

Please don't include private messages unless you choose to.

Free readings

  • Each install has 5 free readings. Each one becomes available again 24 hours after you used it, and the app shows when your next reading will be ready.
  • A reading that fails (for example because of a connection problem) doesn't count.
  • Between Lines also has a daily limit across everyone, and a limit on new installs from the same network. If either is reached, readings pause until the next day (midnight UTC).
  • Reinstalling the app doesn't reset your free readings.
  • Readings need an internet connection. A message can be up to 4,000 characters, and added context up to 1,500.

Common questions

"You've used your free interpretations for now"

You've used all 5. Each one becomes available again 24 hours after you used it; the app shows when the next one is ready.

"Between Lines has reached today's limit" or readings are paused

The daily limit across everyone, or for new installs on your network, has been reached. Please try again after midnight UTC. Your saved people and history aren't affected.

"Couldn't read this message" or "temporarily unavailable"

Check your internet connection and try again in a few minutes. Failed readings don't use up your free readings. If it keeps happening, email us.

"Please reopen Between Lines and try again"

Close Between Lines completely (swipe it away in the app switcher) and open it again.

"We couldn't open your saved people" (or history, or saved replies)

Nothing has been changed or deleted. Close and reopen Between Lines. If the message keeps appearing, email support@nkreative.com before deleting anything.

A reading seems wrong, unkind or harmful

Readings are possibilities, not certainties, and can get things wrong. Under a reading, tap Report a problem with this reading. It opens an email draft to us; it's never sent automatically, and nothing from your reading is added, so you choose what to share.

Can I move my saved people to a new iPhone?

Between Lines has no account or cloud sync, so we can't move or restore your saved people, history or saved replies for you. They're stored only on your device.

Which iPhones are supported?

Between Lines is an iPhone app and needs iOS 15 or later.

Your data and privacy controls

  • AI permission: Between Lines asks before your first reading. Withdraw it at any time on the Privacy page in the app (the Privacy link at the bottom of the home screen). Your saved people and history stay.
  • Delete single items: remove a person, a pattern, a history entry or a saved reply in the app.
  • Delete everything: Delete all local data on the Privacy page permanently removes everyone you've added, their notes and patterns, your reading history, saved replies and your AI permission from your iPhone. It can't be undone.
  • Deleting the app also removes what it saved. Your free-reading count is kept, so it isn't reset by reinstalling.

Our server doesn't store your messages or readings. The Privacy Policy explains exactly what's sent and kept.

For adults 18 and older

Between Lines is intended for adults aged 18 and older. Please do not use the app if you are under 18.

Not a substitute for professional help

Between Lines offers possible readings of messages. It isn't professional, medical, legal or relationship advice, and it isn't an emergency service. If you or someone else is in danger, contact your local emergency services.
