Between Lines for iPhone

Less overthinking.
More understanding.

Paste a message from someone in your life, and Between Lines offers a calm reading of what it might mean, how they might be feeling, and three ways you could reply: gentle, direct, or with a boundary.

Possibilities, not certainties

Readings are thoughtful suggestions, not facts about anyone. You decide what fits, and what to send.

Private by design

  • The people you add, your history and saved replies stay on your iPhone.
  • A message is sent for a reading only after you've given permission, and our server doesn't store it.
  • No account, no ads and no tracking.

Read the Privacy Policy

Between Lines is for adults aged 18 and older.
